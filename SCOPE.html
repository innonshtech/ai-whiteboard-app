The Teacher Mobile App, Student App, Recording, Analytics, and School AI are all out of the AI scope. This document consolidates the full confirmed scope for client sign-off.

✅ Confirmed Scope — In vs Out

✅ In Scope

  • ✅ M1 — Whiteboard Engine (full — all tools, OCR, annotation)
  • ✅ M2 — AI Teacher (IFP Panel only — lesson, quiz, PPT, voice, diagrams)
  • ✅ M3 — Interactive Classroom (polls, raise hand, real-time — no AI)
  • ✅ M4 — Content Library (search + filter + media delivery — no AI)
  • ✅ M5 — Virtual Labs & 3D Models (Three.js — no AI)
  • ✅ M6 — Wireless Casting (WebRTC screen share — no AI)
  • ✅ M7 — Classroom Recording (capture + upload + playback — no AI, no transcript)
  • ✅ M8 — Analytics Dashboard (charts + reports — no AI insights)
  • ✅ M10 — Device Management (license keys, remote lock, health — no AI)
  • ✅ M11 — Cloud Sync (Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, offline — no AI)
  • ✅ M13 — Hardware APIs (touch, stylus, camera, mic — software layer only)
  • ✅ M14 — Security & Auth (JWT, SSO, 2FA, QR, RBAC)
  • ✅ Teacher Mobile App — full CRUD app (no AI endpoints)
  • ✅ Student App — full app (no AI endpoints)
  • ✅ Admin Portal — Super Admin + School Admin (no AI)

🚫 Out of Scope

  • 🚫 M9 — School AI / Private RAG pipeline (entire module removed)
  • 🚫 AI Lesson Planner in Teacher Mobile App
  • 🚫 AI Assistant in Teacher Mobile App (quiz gen, activity suggestions, report card comments)
  • 🚫 Auto-Transcript from classroom recordings (Whisper)
  • 🚫 Auto-Subtitles for recordings
  • 🚫 Auto PDF Notes generated from recordings
  • 🚫 AI-generated insights in Analytics ("Students struggled with Chapter 5")
  • 🚫 Doubt Resolution via AI in Student App
  • 🚫 M15 Future Roadmap (AI Avatar, AI Image/Video Gen, Adaptive Learning)
  • 🚫 ERP Integration

📦 Module-by-Module Scope Detail

🖊️ Module 1 — Whiteboard Engine  ✅ In Scope

  • Infinite canvas, multiple pages, templates, grid mode, dark/light theme
  • All writing tools — pen, pencil, marker, highlighter, brush, laser pointer, eraser, smart eraser
  • Shape recognition, compass, ruler, protractor, graph paper
  • Annotate over PDF, PPT, Word, Excel, images, videos, browser, live camera, screen share
  • OCR — handwriting → text, image → text, PDF → editable text, equations → digital equation
  • Spotlight, screen shade, magnifier, split screen (up to 6)
  • Timer, stopwatch, calculator, periodic table
  • Insert images, videos; Google Image Search, YouTube Search
  • Export — PDF, image, video, IWB format
  • Cloud sync — Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox

🤖 Module 2 — AI Teacher  ✅ In Scope IFP Panel Only

  • AI Lesson Planner — objectives, teaching flow, activities, homework, outcomes
  • AI PPT Generator — generate editable slides, export PPT + PDF
  • AI Explain — beginner / standard / advanced, examples, stories, analogies
  • AI Quiz Generator — MCQ, T/F, fill blanks, HOTS, descriptive, worksheets, difficulty levels
  • AI Worksheet Generator — printable worksheets, homework sheets, practice papers
  • AI Mind Maps — concept maps, flow charts, timelines, relationship maps
  • AI Diagram Generator — text to labeled, editable diagram (e.g. "Digestive System")
  • AI Formula Solver — solve + explain + graph + step-by-step
  • AI Summarizer — revision notes, flash cards, formula sheet, exam notes
  • AI Translator — Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Punjabi, Urdu
  • AI Voice Assistant — "Create quiz", "Next slide", "Explain again", "Generate homework"
🚫 AI Teacher features are panel-only. Teacher Mobile App does not get AI Lesson Planner or AI Assistant.

🎓 Module 3 — Interactive Classroom  ✅ In Scope 🔷 No AI

  • Live Poll — teacher creates, students vote on phone, instant results on panel
  • Quiz participation during live class
  • Raise Hand queue — real-time ordered list on panel
  • Student Whiteboard — each student gets own canvas, teacher reviews
  • Random Student Picker, Emoji Feedback, Attendance via panel
  • Collaborative drawing — multiple students on same canvas simultaneously
  • Challenges — interactive quizzes, group challenges

📚 Module 4 — Content Library  ✅ In Scope 🔷 No AI

  • Search millions of resources — images, videos, diagrams, animations, simulations, worksheets, presentations, question banks
  • Filter by board (CBSE, ICSE, IB, IGCSE, State Boards, JEE, NEET), class, subject, chapter, type
  • Content ingestion pipeline — import GYOASH's content data
  • Content blocking — class-wise age-appropriate filtering (managed by Admin)

🧪 Module 5 — Virtual Labs & 3D Models  ✅ In Scope 🔷 No AI

  • 3D Interactive Models — Solar System, Human Body, DNA, Physics models, Chemistry models (rotate, zoom, animate)
  • Virtual Labs — Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science, Robotics
  • Simulation Engine — drag, drop, experiment, observe, record
  • Gamification — flash cards, puzzles, memory, crossword, word search, spin wheel, leaderboard, badges

📡 Module 6 — Wireless Casting  ✅ In Scope 🔷 No AI

  • Wireless screen casting — Android, iOS, Windows to IFP panel
  • Device discovery on local network, QR or PIN pairing

🎥 Module 7 — Classroom Recording  ✅ In Scope 🔷 No AI

  • Record whiteboard + screen + teacher audio
  • Auto-upload to cloud storage (S3/Azure), chunked upload
  • Video encoding, thumbnails, playback optimisation
  • Students watch recordings on app — resume, speed control, bookmark, download
🚫 Removed: Auto-transcript (Whisper), auto-subtitles (SRT), AI-generated PDF notes from recording — all out of scope.

📊 Module 8 — Analytics Dashboard  ✅ In Scope 🔷 No AI

  • Teacher Dashboard — lessons delivered, topics covered, quiz results, student participation, attendance
  • School Dashboard — device health, teacher usage, classroom utilisation, learning reports
  • Export reports — PDF and Excel
🚫 Removed: AI-generated text insights (e.g. "Students struggled with Chapter 5") — out of scope. Pure data aggregation and charting only.

🧠 Module 9 — School AI / Private RAG  🚫 Removed

  • Per-school private AI knowledge base
  • Upload books, PDFs, notes, question banks, policies, previous papers, ERP data
  • AI answers only from school-approved content
  • Vector DB per school, embedding pipeline, RAG query pipeline
🚫 This entire module is removed from the current engagement scope.

🔧 Module 10 — Device Management  ✅ In Scope

  • Super Admin — school registration, branch registration, classroom-wise panel mapping
  • License key generation (one per panel), key validity management, key extension
  • Remote lock / unlock panels, remote app install, health reports, device monitoring
  • Cost master, log monitoring

☁️ Module 11 — Cloud Sync  ✅ In Scope

  • Auto save to cloud, Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox integration
  • Offline mode, version history
  • Client-wise and branch-wise cloud storage mapping

⚙️ Module 13 — Hardware Integration  ✅ In Scope

  • Software APIs for camera (Bluetooth + inbuilt), microphones, speakers
  • Touch input handling, stylus pressure (PointerEvent API), palm rejection
  • Gesture control (software layer) — hardware provided by GYOASH

🔒 Module 14 — Security & Auth  ✅ In Scope

  • Email login, OTP, Google SSO, 2FA (TOTP), QR code login
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) — Super Admin, School Admin, Teacher, Student/Parent
  • Audit logs, encrypted sessions, profanity filters
  • School-wise language mapping, AI plan mapping per school

📱 Teacher Mobile App  ✅ In Scope 🔷 No AI

  • Dashboard — today's timetable, upcoming classes, pending homework, notifications
  • My Classes — class list, sections, student list, class schedule
  • Attendance — mark, edit, view history, absent list, leave status, reports
  • Homework — create, attach files, set due date, view submissions, add remarks
  • Assessments — create tests, define marks, schedule, enter marks, publish results
  • Content Repository — lesson plans, worksheets, question papers, search by subject/class/chapter
  • Student Profiles — attendance, marks, assignment status, behaviour notes, parent contact
  • Communication — announcements, individual messages, parent messages, circulars
  • Calendar, Timetable, Reports (export PDF/Excel), Notifications, Teacher Profile, Settings
🚫 Removed from Teacher App: AI Lesson Planner (Section 6 of Annex C), AI Assistant (Section 14 of Annex C) — AI quiz gen, homework gen, activity suggestions, report card comments. These features are available on the IFP Panel only.

📱 Student App  ✅ In Scope 🔷 No AI

  • Login — parent mobile/email, OTP, Google login; multi-child switch
  • Dashboard — timetable, upcoming classes, homework due, announcements, attendance summary
  • Online Classes — join live class, raise hand, voice question, chat, emoji, polls, quiz participation
  • Recorded Classes — watch, resume, speed control, download, bookmark, search
  • Homework — view, submit (photos/PDF/audio/video), track status, view feedback
  • Assessments — MCQ, subjective, true/false, fill blanks, match the following
  • Quiz & Puzzles — brain games, crossword, word search, match pairs, memory, logic puzzles, STEM challenges
  • Gamification — points, badges, leaderboard, streaks, achievement levels
  • Learning Resources — class notes, PDFs, PPTs, videos, worksheets, previous year papers
  • Academic Performance — test results, assignment marks, progress charts, teacher feedback
  • Timetable, Calendar, Announcements, Notifications, Settings, Security
🚫 Removed from Student App: AI-powered Doubt Resolution (Section 11 of Annex D — ask by voice, upload image, get AI response). Replaced with a simple teacher-reply thread (no AI).

❓ Open Questions — Awaiting Client Confirmation

💡 These are the only unresolved points. Once you confirm these, the scope is locked and development can begin.

1
AI Lesson Planner — Panel only, or also in Teacher Mobile App?
Annex C (Teacher App spec) Section 6 explicitly lists AI Lesson Planner as a Teacher App feature. Your current instruction says AI only in the whiteboard. Please confirm whether the AI Lesson Planner should be accessible from the Teacher's phone, or restricted to the IFP panel only.
Option A — Panel only (no AI on Teacher App) Option B — Teacher App also gets AI Lesson Planner
2
Doubt Resolution in Student App — remove completely, or replace with teacher-reply thread?
Annex D Section 11 shows students asking questions by voice, image upload, getting AI responses. Since AI is removed from Student App, do you want to keep a manual doubt resolution feature (student asks → teacher replies), or remove it entirely?
Option A — Remove completely Option B — Keep as manual teacher-reply thread (no AI)
3
Module 7 Recording — is the raw video recording still required, or is recording itself out of scope?
Annex B Module 7 listed Recording along with transcript, subtitles, and PDF notes. Since the AI outputs (transcript, subtitles, PDF notes) are removed, is basic video recording (capture + upload + student playback) still needed? Or is Module 7 removed entirely?
Option A — Keep basic recording (no transcript/subtitles/PDF) Option B — Remove Module 7 entirely
4
Module 9 (School AI / Private RAG) — confirmed removed, or deferred to a later phase?
School AI was a key differentiator in the original spec (Annex B Module 9). Please confirm whether it is removed from the current engagement entirely, or should be treated as a future phase (Phase 3) to be scoped and priced separately at a later date.
Option A — Removed entirely from this project Option B — Deferred to Phase 3 (separate scope + pricing)
